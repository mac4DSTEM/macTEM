## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-23

First release. macTEM analyses TEM images, EDS and EELS spectrum images and diffraction patterns on Apple silicon
Macs with macOS 27. Rooms: Image, FFT, EDS, EELS, Diffraction and Results. The numbers come from the suite's kits
(FormatoKit 0.11.0, DiffractoKit 0.10.0, ImagoKit 0.7.0, SpectroKit 0.12.0, MacEMUI 0.3.0); anything not yet checked
against known truth says `validation: none`.

Migration: none, first release.
