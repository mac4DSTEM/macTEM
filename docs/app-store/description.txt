macTEM analyses TEM images, EDS and EELS spectrum images and electron diffraction patterns natively on Apple silicon. Open a file, look, measure, and export a figure, with every number traceable to the method it came from.

OPEN YOUR FILES
DigitalMicrograph (.dm3, .dm4), Velox (.emd), TIFF and MRC, and single .msa spectra. The file's content decides where it opens: an image in the Image room, a spectrum image in the EDS or EELS room. A multi-page TIFF or ImageJ stack opens frame by frame, read on demand.

ROOMS
• Image: calibration from the file, colormap, contrast and gamma; distances, angles, line profiles and particles; image stacks. Your marks are saved in a session file next to the image.
• FFT: the transform of the image or a region with a window, spots and their d-spacings, the radial profile, a masked inverse, and Gaussian, band-pass, ABSF and Wiener filters.
• EDS: Velox and GMS spectrum images. Pick elements from the periodic table or let Auto ID suggest them, then get net-count, wt%, at% and thickness maps, regions, and the Cliff-Lorimer composition, with zeta-factor and cross-section methods.
• EELS: Dual-EELS spectrum images. Align the zero-loss peak, map t/λ, pick edges from 383 K, L and M edges of 95 elements, and fit their intensities and at%, each with its uncertainty.
• Diffraction: calibrate on a ring of a standard (Au, Al or Si), then index rings and spots against a standard or a CIF and read their d-spacings.
• Results: every measurement of a file in one table, exported as CSV.

Exports are PNG figures with the scale bar, CSV tables, MSA spectra, and a method JSON for the spectroscopy rooms.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM
Every number is computed by the open macEM Swift packages, each checked against the Python package it ports, within that package's own tolerance: file readers, FFT and image maths, crystallography, and EDS and EELS. EELS cross sections are the generalised oscillator strengths of Segger, Guzzinati and Kohl, bundled with the app.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR MAC
No account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no network access. Your files are read from and written to your own storage only.

OPEN SOURCE
macTEM is free software under GPL-3.0. The source and the issue tracker are at github.com/mac4DSTEM/macTEM.

Its companion app, mac4DSTEM, analyses 4D-STEM data: virtual images, strain, orientation, ptychography and EDS.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon.
