App Privacy (App Store Connect)

Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? No.
Label shown on the store: Data Not Collected.

Why this is correct:
- macEM makes no network connection. Its sandbox entitlements are the App Sandbox, user-selected file read-write and app-scoped bookmarks; it has no network client entitlement.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or tracking identifiers.
- Files, sessions and exports are read from and written to the user's own storage only, reached through the system Open and Save panels.
