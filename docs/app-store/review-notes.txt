Notes for App Review

macTEM analyses transmission electron microscope images, EDS and EELS spectrum images and diffraction patterns. You do not need data of your own to try it.

How to try it:
1. Launch the app.
2. Choose Help > Open Demo Data and pick one of the generated documents: Lattice Image, Au Diffraction Pattern, EDX Spectrum Image or EELS Spectrum Image. Each opens in its room.
3. Work down the sidebar: Image (measure a distance, change the contrast), FFT (the transform and its spots), Diffraction (calibrate on the ring pattern), EDX and EELS (spectra, maps and quantification on the demo spectrum images), Results (every measurement in one table, exported as CSV).

No account, sign-in, purchase or network connection is needed anywhere in the app. macTEM makes no network requests.
