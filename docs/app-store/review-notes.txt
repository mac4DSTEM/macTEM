macTEM analyses transmission electron microscope images, EDS and EELS spectrum images and diffraction patterns. You do not need data of your own to try it.

How to try it:
1. Launch the app.
2. [Open the built-in demo: menu item to be named, see the note above.]
3. Work down the sidebar: Image (measure a distance, change the contrast), FFT (the transform and its spots), Diffraction (calibrate on the ring pattern), Results (every measurement in one table, exported as CSV).

No account, sign-in, purchase or network connection is needed anywhere in the app. macTEM makes no network requests.
